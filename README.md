# MA-Summer-Paper
UBC Economics MA summer paper studying the effects of pension vesting on party loyalty in the US House of Representatives. Awarded the W. Erwin Diewert Graduate Prize in Applied Economics.

Replication instructions and the master file to run all scripts are in the replication folder. The paper itself is 'Summer paper.pdf'.

This research combines a dynamic programming model of legislator decisions with an econometric analysis of House roll call voting patterns from 1987-2024. Solving the model analytically and numerically yields a prediction of a distinctive vesting incentive effect on party loyalty (how often a legislator votes with their party). The empirical results do not match this prediction, but reveal a robust pattern of elevated party loyalty in Representatives' early careers.
